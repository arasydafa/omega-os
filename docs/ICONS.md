## Rules

1. Never use emoji in UI.
2. `trash-2` and maroon are destructive-only.
3. `triangle-alert` is warning-only (yellow), `octagon-x` is error-only (maroon).
4. Toast and alert share the same 4 status colors: info blue `#1D4ED8`, warning yellow `#B45309`, success green `#15803D`, danger maroon `#7B1E26` (light mode).
